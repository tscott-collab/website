Our Story

Built from a love of the natural world.

Stone Creek was built from a love of the natural world—its textures, landscapes, weather, and the places that stay with us long after we’ve left them.

Every fragrance begins with observation. A forest after rain. A weathered dock. An overgrown orchard. Freshly turned earth. An old bakery before sunrise. We don’t chase trends or seasonal themes. We capture moments that have a distinct sense of place.

Our candles are hand-poured in small batches using thoughtfully selected fragrances, simple materials, and an approach rooted in deliberate restraint. We believe the most memorable scents don’t simply smell good—they transport you somewhere real.

How We Work

Deliberate restraint, in every pour.

Hand-poured, small batch

No mass production. Every candle is poured, checked, and finished by hand in limited runs.

Thoughtfully selected fragrance

Every note earns its place. We test, edit, and wait until a scent feels true before it’s ever bottled.

Simple materials, no clutter

Clean wax, clean vessels, honest labeling. Nothing added just to fill space on a shelf.
